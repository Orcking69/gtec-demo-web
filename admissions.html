Admissions

Admissions

Eligibility, application steps and sample fees for the four-year BSc in Textile Engineering. [SAMPLE FIGURES throughout — confirm against the real circular.]

Eligibility (sample)

  • HSC (Science group) with a minimum combined GPA — see the current circular
  • Passed HSC/equivalent within the last two academic sessions
  • Valid national ID or birth registration
  • Meets seat-specific quota conditions where applicable

Application steps

  1. Complete the online application form
  2. Upload SSC & HSC certificates and transcripts
  3. Pay the application fee
  4. Sit for the joint admission test (MCQ, based on HSC syllabus)
  5. Confirm seat via merit list & complete enrolment
