About GTEC

A short institutional history, our affiliation, and a welcome message from the principal.

Our history

Gopalganj Textile Engineering College began academic activities in March 2021, one of the government textile engineering colleges established under the Directorate of Textiles to expand BSc-level textile education outside Dhaka. GTEC offers a direct four-year Bachelor of Science in Textile Engineering — students are admitted straight into the degree programme from HSC, with no separate diploma stage.

The college is organised into four specialised departments — Yarn Engineering, Fabric Engineering, Wet Process Engineering and Apparel Engineering — each admitting around 30 students a year, for a total undergraduate intake of roughly 120 students annually. Affiliation

BUTEX affiliation

GTEC is one of the government textile engineering colleges academically affiliated with the Bangladesh University of Textiles (BUTEX), which sets the curriculum and confers the BSc in Textile Engineering degree. Administratively, the college operates under the Directorate of Textiles, Ministry of Textiles and Jute.
